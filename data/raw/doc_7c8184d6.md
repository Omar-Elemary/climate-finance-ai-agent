### Europe’s leading **climate innovation agency** and community
Stichting Climate-KIC International Foundation is an independent, non-profit with public benefit status (ANBI). It is registered in the Netherlands, with branches in several European countries and operating across the world. The foundation is focused on implementing the Climate KIC strategy by delivering our projects and programmes, and building our community and collaborative efforts. Its objective is to contribute to the protection of nature and the environment via innovation of climate change mitigation and adaptation.
