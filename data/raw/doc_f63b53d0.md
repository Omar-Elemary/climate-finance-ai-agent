About Us
Fund for responding to Loss and Damage (FRLD) addresses the urgent and growing needs of vulnerable communities in developing countries facing the irreversible impacts of climate change. It finances initiatives to help vulnerable communities recover from climate-related losses and damage resulting from incidents such as climate-induced extreme weather events, rising sea levels, and other climate-induced crises. These initiatives are tailored to respond directly to country-specific needs and priorities, ensuring that solutions are locally driven and contextually appropriate.  
  
[Learn more](https://www.frld.org/about)
FRLD Board
The Board of the Fund for responding to Loss and Damage consists of 26 members from Parties to the Convention and Paris Agreement, with 12 members from developed country Parties and 14 from developing country Parties. The geographical representation includes 12 members from developed countries, three from Asia-Pacific States, three from African States, three from Latin American and Caribbean States, two from small island developing States, two from the least developed countries, and one from a developing country not included in the previously listed groups. Each Board member has an alternate. In January 2026, the Board elected Ms. Camila Minerva Rodriguez Tavarez from the Dominican Republic, and Mr. Georg Børsting from Norway as the FRLD's Co-Chairs for a one-year term or until their successors have been elected. Strong leadership and governance underscores the Board's commitment to fostering collaborative and inclusive decision-making as the FRLD responds to the critical challenge of climate-induced loss and damage.
