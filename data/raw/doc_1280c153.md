# National energy and climate plans
EU countries’ 10-year national energy and climate plans for 2021-2030. 
Page contents 
Page contents
  * [The process](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/strategy/energy-union/national-energy-and-climate-plans_en#the-process)
  * [National Energy and Climate plans 2021-2030](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/strategy/energy-union/national-energy-and-climate-plans_en#national-energy-and-climate-plans-2021-2030)
  * [Related links](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/strategy/energy-union/national-energy-and-climate-plans_en#related-links)


The National Energy and Climate Plans (NECPs) are an essential strategic planning tool to deliver a fair, resilient, and climate-neutral Europe, and to steer the much-needed investments for the climate and energy transition, helping to mobilise private and public spending.
The NECPs enable each EU country to outline precise actions and policies to achieve the 2030 climate and energy targets and fulfil the broader objectives of the Energy Union.
Required under the [Governance Regulation (EU)2018/1999](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.2018.328.01.0001.01.ENG&toc=OJ:L:2018:328:TOC), the NECPs first took shape in 2020 and have since then been updated to incorporate the ambitious targets set by the European Green Deal, the Fit for 55 Package legislation and the RepowerEU plan.
![](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2023-01/necp_web_twitterfomat.jpg?itok=Ao4GZjAO)
©iStock/Bim/Kapook2981/Metemorworks/Ridofranz
The NECPs define how EU countries will tackle the 5 dimensions of the Energy Union:
  * decarbonisation
  * energy efficiency
  * energy security
  * internal energy market
  * research, innovation and competitiveness


These plans necessitate coordinated efforts across government departments, creating a robust framework that catalyses public and private investment and supports a thorough implementation of the agreed policies. 
## The process
Under the Governance Regulation EU countries have to submit their [NECPs](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/publications/draft-national-energy-and-climate-plans-necps-submitted-2018_en) for the period 2021-2030 to the Commission. Each country shall also submit a progress report every 2 years, according to the structure, format, technical details and process set out in the [Implementing Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R2299). The Commission will, as part of the [state of the energy union report](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-strategy/energy-union_en#state-of-the-energy-union-reports), monitor the EU's progress as a whole towards achieving these targets.
To better develop and implement the plans, EU countries were required to consult citizens, businesses and regional authorities in the drafting and finalisation process.
They submitted their final NECPs in 2019 and then updated them in 2024, to reflect the increased EU 2030 climate and energy ambitions, as set by the Fit for 55 legislative package and the RepowerEU plan.
The governance regulation also required EU countries to submit by the start of 2020 national [long term strategies](https://commission.europa.eu/energy-climate-change-environment/implementation-eu-countries/energy-and-climate-governance-and-reporting/national-long-term-strategies_en) looking forward to 2050.
From 2021, EU countries are required to submit information on climate and energy progress via dedicated [reporting platforms](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/strategy/energy-union/reporting-system-eu-countries_en).
NECPs as a governance tool will be reviewed for the post-2030 period as part of the forthcoming [revision of the governance of the Energy Union and climate action](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/16034-Update-of-the-governance-of-the-Energy-Union-and-climate-action_en).

### Initial NECPs 2021-2030
Under the Governance Regulation EU countries had to submit their draft NECPs for the period 2021-2030 to the Commission by 31 December 2018. These were analysed by the Commission with an [overall assessment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1565713062913&uri=CELEX:52019DC0285) and country-specific recommendations published in June 2019. Taking these recommendations into account, EU countries were then required to submit their final NECPs by 31 December 2019.
On 17 September 2020, the Commission published a detailed [EU-wide assessment of the final NECPs](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1600339518571&uri=COM%3A2020%3A564%3AFIN). As a follow-up, and as part of the 2020 Energy Union report, the Commission published [individual assessments](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/individual-assessments_en) of each of the national plans with further guidance.
